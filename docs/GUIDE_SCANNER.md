# Canteen Agent — Scanner Guide

A one-page guide for the person scanning badges at the canteen door.

## Before lunch
1. Open the app on the tablet/phone and sign in with the **scanner** account.
2. You land on the **Scanner** screen. Check the top row:
   - the **service** should say *Lunch*,
   - the connection dot: green = online, orange = local, red = offline (still works, queues).
3. Tap **▶ Start camera** and allow camera access (first time only).

## Scanning
- Hold each employee's badge in front of the camera. You do **not** press any button.
- Watch the big panel:
  - 🟢 **MEAL AUTHORIZED** + name → let them through (you'll hear a short beep / feel a buzz).
  - 🔴 **MEAL ALREADY RECORDED** → they already ate today; it shows their first passage time.
  - 🟠 **ACCESS NOT AUTHORIZED** → not eligible (e.g. not an Agent). Show them the reason.
  - 🔴 **INVALID OR REVOKED BADGE** → badge not recognised; use Manual ID or send to HR.
- The camera re-arms automatically for the next person. The **session counter** shows how
  many meals you've served.

## If a badge won't scan
1. Tap **⌨ Manual ID**.
2. Type the Employee ID — the name and department appear.
3. Tap **Look up & confirm**. The same checks apply. It's recorded as a manual entry
   (HR can see manual entries in the reports).

## Notes
- Do not share your login. Tap **⎋ Sign out** when your shift ends.
- If the internet drops, keep scanning — meals are saved and sync when it returns.
  For guaranteed no-duplicates across several devices, only one terminal should scan
  while offline.
- You cannot change employees, prices, or cancel meals — that's HR's job.
